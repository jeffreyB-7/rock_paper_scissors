# Rock Paper Scissors: Plan

## Goal
A simple browser game made with plain HTML, CSS, and JavaScript, where you play Rock Paper Scissors against the computer and keep a running score.

## Who it is for
Classmates and friends who want a quick, casual game in the browser. (Also submitted as a CS 191 class assignment.)

## Must-have features
1. **Play a round**: click Rock, Paper, or Scissors. The computer picks at random, and the app shows both picks and the result (win, lose, or tie).
2. **Running score**: counts of wins, losses, and ties that update after every round.
3. **Reset button**: sets the score back to zero without reloading the page.

## Nice-to-haves
1. **Inspirational quotes** (top priority): a scrolling ticker of quotes along the bottom of the page that slides from right to left. Quote data to be provided later.
2. **Sounds and animations**: sound effects, a countdown, or a shake before the reveal.
3. **Smarter computer**: a difficulty mode that learns from the player's past picks instead of picking at random.

## Out of scope
- Round history list
- Keyboard controls
- Saving the score between visits
- Best-of-N matches
- Multiplayer or online play
- Frameworks, libraries, or build tools
